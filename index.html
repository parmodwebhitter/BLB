What Is Link Building in SEO?

Written by [Founder Name], Head of Link Building Sample · Last updated: October 2026

Link building in SEO is the process of getting other trusted websites to link to your website. Google treats each quality backlink as a vote of confidence, so the more relevant, authoritative sites that link to you, the more credible your site looks and the higher it can rank.

But not every link helps. Links from spammy or unrelated sites can do nothing, or even hurt your rankings. That's why a good link building strategy focuses on quality, relevance and natural placement, not just numbers.

What Is Dofollow Link Building?

Dofollow link building is the process of earning backlinks that do not carry a rel="nofollow", rel="sponsored" or rel="ugc" tag, so they pass link equity to your website and directly improve your rankings and Domain Rating. Done right, it means contextual dofollow links placed inside relevant content on real, high-traffic websites.

Why Dofollow Links Matter

They are the links that pass ranking power. Most of the ranking gain from link building comes from quality dofollow links.

How We Build Them

Guest posts and niche edits on vetted sites, with your link placed naturally in the body content, not in footers, sidebars or author bios.

Keep the Mix Natural

A profile with 100% dofollow links looks unnatural to Google. We keep a healthy mix with some nofollow and branded links.

Step 1Link Building Basics
01

How Does Link Building Help SEO?

Each quality backlink is a vote of trust that helps your pages rank higher, get more keywords on page one and bring more organic traffic. See exactly how backlinks help SEO ↑

02

What Is Broken Link Building in SEO?

Broken link building means finding dead (404) links on relevant websites and offering your working page as a replacement. The site owner fixes a broken link for their readers, and you earn a natural, editorial backlink. It works best when your content is a close match for the page that went missing.

03

What Is Contextual Link Building?

Contextual link building means getting your link placed inside the main body of a relevant article, surrounded by text on the same topic. Google values these links far more than footer, sidebar or author-bio links, because the context shows the link is a genuine recommendation.

04

What Is an Editorial Link?

An editorial link is a backlink that a site's editor or writer chooses to include because your content genuinely adds value for their readers. It sits naturally inside the article, is not paid for or forced, and is one of the strongest trust signals Google looks at. Manual outreach with useful content is how we earn them for you.

Step 2Building Links Safely
05

What Is a White-Hat Approach to Building Backlinks?

A white-hat approach follows Google's guidelines: you earn links through useful content, personal outreach and genuine relationships with real websites. It avoids PBNs, link farms, automated spam and bulk paid links. See how we choose sites.

06

How to Build Backlinks Safely?

Vet every site for real traffic, niche relevance and a low spam score, keep your anchor text natural and mostly branded, and build links at a steady pace that suits your site's age. Avoid PBNs and link packages that promise hundreds of links overnight, and monitor your profile so you can replace any link that drops.

07

How to Build Local Backlinks for SEO?

Start with consistent local citations and business directories, then earn links from local news sites, chambers of commerce, suppliers, sponsored events and community blogs. Local links tell Google you are a trusted business in your area, which helps you show up in Google Maps and "near me" searches.

Step 3Links for AI Search
08

How to Build Backlinks for AI Search Visibility?

AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews lean on sources that trusted sites already mention. Earn links and brand mentions on authoritative, topic-relevant websites, get listed in industry roundups and "best of" articles, and keep your brand name, services and expertise consistent everywhere so AI tools understand and cite you.

Step 4Choosing an Agency
09

How to Evaluate Backlink Building Services for SEO Agencies?

Ask to see sample sites with their DR, traffic and niche, check that links are placed in real content (not PBNs), and confirm you get white-label reports with every URL. Look for a replacement guarantee, clear delivery times and the option to approve sites before outreach. See our white label link building.

10

How to Hire a Link Building Agency?

Shortlist agencies with real case studies and verified reviews, then ask how they find and vet sites, whether you approve each one, how they report and what happens if a link is removed. Avoid anyone guaranteeing #1 rankings or hundreds of cheap links. A good agency starts with a free audit and a clear plan. See our packages.
